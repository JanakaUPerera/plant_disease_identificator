What does the percentage mean?

It is the model's estimated chance that the answer is right. The percentages were calibrated on real-world validation photos (PlantDoc): across those photos, when the model said about 60%, it was right about 60% of the time. On such photos it is right about half the time overall, so treat every answer as a suggestion to confirm, never as a diagnosis.
